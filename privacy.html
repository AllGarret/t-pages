YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

This page describes how information is handled when you use Tabi.

Information used by Tabi

When you create or access a Tabi account, your email address and authentication data are processed to provide account access. Tabi uses Supabase Authentication for email and password authentication.

Information you choose to submit through Tabi may be processed to provide the features you request.

How information is used

Account information is used to create and secure your account, authenticate sign-ins, and support the operation of Tabi. Supabase processes authentication information as a service provider under its own privacy and security practices.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account. For requests about information associated with your account, contact the Tabi service operator through the support channel provided with the service.

Updates to this page

This page may be updated as Tabi’s features and data practices change. Please review it periodically for the current information.
